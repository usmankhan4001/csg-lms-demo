# Business Requirements: Embedded AI Business Value & ROI Framework

**Requirement ID:** BFR-AI-001  
**Domain:** Embedded Artificial Intelligence  
**Stakeholders:** Board of Directors, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Academic Officer, School Principals  
**Classification:** Pure Business Requirements Specification  

---

## 1. Business Need & Strategic Premise

Artificial Intelligence in education has frequently been implemented as a gimmick—such as generic conversational chatbots that hallucinate answers or generate essays for students. In an enterprise educational institution, AI must serve as an **operational force multiplier** that delivers quantifiable business ROI, eliminates administrative drudgery for teachers, accelerates student recruitment, and enhances genuine student cognitive mastery.

The **Embedded AI Business Value & ROI Framework** establishes the strategic governance, business metrics, and return-on-investment parameters for all artificial intelligence capabilities embedded across the CSG-LMS platform.

## 3. Pedagogical & Ethical Guardrails

### 3.1 The Socratic Scaffolding Mandate (No Direct Answers)
- When a student asks the AI tutor for homework help (e.g., "What is the answer to question 4?"):
  - The AI is **architecturally barred** from providing direct answers, full solutions, or writing essays.
  - The AI must employ **Socratic Scaffolding**: asking guiding questions, identifying underlying conceptual misconceptions, and referencing specific textbook chapters.

### 3.2 Universal 2-Minute Emergency Crisis Escalation SLA
- Any student conversation containing despair keywords, suicidal ideation, or domestic abuse indicators triggers an **instant emergency protocol**:
  1. AI immediately halts generation and displays verified national crisis helpline numbers.
  2. The system dispatches a high-priority, quiet-hours-overriding alert to the designated certified school psychologist within a strict 120-second SLA.
  3. The conversation is flagged for mandatory clinical review.

## 4. Business Value & Strategic Impact
- **Substantial Administrative ROI:** Reclaims thousands of staff hours annually across scheduling, admissions, and grading.
- **Sustainable Faculty Retention:** Reduces teacher burnout by cutting weekend grading burdens in half.
- **Differentiated Institutional Value Proposition:** Provides prospective parents with a compelling reason to choose the school: every enrolled student receives a personalized, curriculum-grounded Socratic tutor.
