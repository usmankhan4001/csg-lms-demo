## 1. Lifecycle Overview

An educational institution operates as a continuous, multi-year progression. The student journey spans eight interconnected operational phases: from prospective family outreach to alumni engagement. 

CSG-LMS seamlessly automates the handshakes between these phases, eliminating administrative silos and paperwork.

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Operational Mechanics

### Phase 1: Lead Inquiry & Recruitment
- Inquiring parents engage across web forms, WhatsApp, or walk-in inquiries.
- The 24/7 Conversational AI Agent captures basic demographics, answers curriculum questions, verifies age eligibility, and books guided campus tours.

### Phase 2: Admissions Assessment & Offer Issuance
- The prospective student completes entrance evaluations (online or proctored).
- Admissions panels score interviews and academic transcripts.
- An official Admissions Offer is generated containing customized fee quotations, applicable scholarship deductions, and electronic enrollment contracts.

### Phase 3: Automated Matriculation & Onboarding (The Missing Bridge)
- **The Handshake:** Upon offer acceptance and registration deposit verification, the system triggers the automated **Matriculation Handshake**:
  1. Promotes the applicant to an active **Matriculated Student**.
  2. Issues an institutional Student ID and automated Roll Number.
  3. Establishes the **Family Network**, linking parents with custody and emergency contact priority.
  4. Enrolls the student into their designated **Academic Program** and **Batch / Section**.
  5. Automatically generates the student's **Term Fee Schedule & Tuition Invoices**.

### Phase 4: Daily Academic Operations
- The student follows an optimized, conflict-free weekly timetable.
- Homeroom and subject teachers mark period-by-period batch attendance via the 1-Click Attendance Tool.
- Unexcused absences immediately trigger automated notifications to primary guardians.
- Finance tracks incoming installment payments against automated payment schedules.

### Phase 5: Curriculum Delivery & Grounded AI Coaching
- Teachers deliver rich multimedia lessons, interactive exercises, and collaborative whiteboards via LearnHouse content engines.
- Students interact with the course-grounded Socratic AI Tutor outside classroom hours, receiving hints and explanations strictly aligned with their weekly syllabus topics.

### Phase 6: Assessment, Weighted Grading & Report Cards
- Students submit assignments, lab reports, and sit for computer-based examinations.
- The system computes weighted term grades according to the course's approved Assessment Plan (e.g., Continuous Assessment 40% + Midterm 20% + Final 40%).
- Official, printable term report cards with teacher narratives and principal digital signatures are generated.

### Phase 7: Annual Academic Progression & Rollover
- At the end of the academic year, the registrar executes the **Mass Promotion Tool**.
- Passing students are automatically promoted into the next grade level batch (e.g., Grade 9-A $\to$ Grade 10-A), while credit deficiencies trigger remedial summer enrollment.
- Next-year tuition fee structures are scheduled.

### Phase 8: Graduation, Transcripts & Alumni Network
- The system runs an automated Graduation Credit Audit verifying all mandatory program credits, GPA thresholds, and community service hours.
- Generates tamper-evident official transcripts and digital diplomas.
- Transitions the student into the permanent institutional Alumni Directory.
